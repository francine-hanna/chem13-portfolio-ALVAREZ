AI USE DISCLOSURE

AI tools were used as supplementary aids throughout the preparation of this activity. I used ChatGPT to brainstorm ideas, organize information, improve wording, summarize concepts, formalize selected text, and assist with the molecular structure visualization. I also used Gemini to combine information, summarize ideas, and suggest alternative terminology. All AI-assisted information was reviewed and verified using reliable references before being included in the final work. The final content, design, organization, and presentation were independently reviewed, edited, and finalized by me.
